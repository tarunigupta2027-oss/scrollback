How do I get the chat file out of WhatsApp?
  1. Open the chat in WhatsApp.
  2. Tap the contact or group name at the top.
  3. Scroll right down and tap Export Chat, then Without Media.
  4. In the share sheet, swipe across the grey icons and tap Save to Files. Pick On My iPhone.
  5. Come back here, tap the box above, tap Browse, and pick the file you just saved.
  1. Open the chat in WhatsApp.
  2. Tap the three dots, top right, then More, then Export chat.
  3. Choose Without media.
  4. Pick Files (or Drive) to save it. It usually lands in Downloads.
  5. Come back here, tap the box above, and pick it from Downloads or Recent.

Stuck? You can also share the export to any app that keeps files — Telegram saved messages, Notes, your own email. Then open it from there.
